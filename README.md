## Official & Planning

Essential government entry portals, tourism boards, visa requirements, and meteorological updates.

- [Visit Czechia (CzechTourism)](https://www.visitczechia.com/) - Official national tourist authority portal offering destination guides, regional routes, and cultural highlights.
- [Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MZV)](https://www.mzv.cz/jnp/en/index.html) - Official government portal for Schengen visa entry rules, long-term permits, and consular services.
- [Nature Conservation Agency (AOPK ČR)](https://www.nature.cz/) - Official administration authority managing national parks, protected landscape areas (CHKO), and outdoor reserves.
- [CHMI Weather (Český hydrometeorologický ústav)](https://www.chmi.cz/) - Official Czech Hydrometeorological Institute for weather forecasts, river levels, and storm alerts.

## Transportation

Navigating national railway lines, intercity bus carriers, city transit apps, and air routes.

- [Můj Vlak (České dráhy)](https://www.cd.cz/en/) - Official Czech Railways journey planner and ticketing portal for domestic and international train lines.
- [RegioJet](https://www.regiojet.com/) - Major private operator running yellow long-distance coaches and passenger train services across Czechia and Central Europe.
- [Leo Express](https://www.leoexpress.com/en) - Rail and bus service provider linking major Czech hubs with neighboring EU destinations.
- [IDOS Timetables](https://idos.idnes.cz/en/) - National multimodal public transit planner covering all domestic trains, buses, and municipal transport networks.
- [Prague Airport (Václav Havel Airport)](https://www.prg.aero/en) - Primary international aviation gateway with route information, transport links, and terminal services.

## Destinations & Regions

### Prague & Central Bohemia

- [Prague.eu (Official Tourist Website)](https://www.prague.eu/en) - Official capital city guide for exploring Prague Castle, Charles Bridge, museums, and neighborhood circuits.
- [PID Litáčka](https://pid.cz/en/) - Official integrated transport app and ticketing system for Prague subway, trams, buses, and ferries.
- [Central Bohemia Tourism](https://www.centralbohemia.cz/) - Regional guide for Karlštejn Castle, Kutná Hora (Sedlec Ossuary), and Křivoklátsko.

### South Bohemia

- [South Bohemia Tourism](https://www.suedbohmen.info/en) - Official portal for exploring Český Krumlov, České Budějovice, and the Šumava National Park.

### West Bohemia & Spa Triangle

- [Živý Kraj (Karlovy Vary Region)](https://www.zivykraj.cz/en/) - Visitor guide covering the historic spa towns of Karlovy Vary, Mariánské Lázně, and Františkovy Lázně.
- [Pilsen Tourism](https://www.pilsen.eu/en/) - Travel guide for the home of Pilsner Urquell, historical underground tunnels, and West Bohemian heritage.

### North & East Bohemia

- [Bohemian Switzerland National Park (České Švýcarsko)](https://www.npcs.cz/en) - Official park portal detailing hiking routes, Pravčická brána, and gorge boat trips.
- [Krkonoše National Park (KRNAP)](https://www.krnap.cz/en/) - Official website for Czechia's highest mountain range, Sněžka peak, and winter sport resorts.

### Moravia & Wine Regions

- [Go To Brno](https://www.gotobrno.cz/en/) - Official municipal travel portal for Brno, Villa Tugendhat, underground catacombs, and local coffee culture.
- [Wine of Czech Republic](https://www.wineofczechrepublic.cz/) - Official guide to South Moravian wine trails, wine cellars, and harvest festivals.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for extended stays, self-employed workers (Živno), and foreign remote professionals.

- [Digital Nomad Program (Ministry of Industry and Trade)](https://www.mpo.cz/en/) - Official government program enabling streamlined visa processing for IT professionals and remote workers.
- [Czech Trade Licensing Register (Živnostenský rejstřík)](https://www.rzp.cz/) - Official portal for managing and verifying Živnostenský list (freelance trade license) records.

## Safety & Local Etiquette

Practical guidance on emergency lines, currency rules, and trail markers.

- [Czech Hiking Markers (KČT)](https://kct.cz/) - Official Czech Tourist Club trail system guide, renowned for its color-coded hiking path network.
- [112 European Emergency Number](https://www.hzscr.cz/) - Integrated national emergency dispatch line for police, fire, and medical help across the Czech Republic.
